A SMALL TOOL FOR BETTER HANDOFFS

See it. Capture it. Keep it.

On an HTTP or HTTPS page, open the IssueCapture Chrome toolbar button and choose Capture this page. Or use ⌘⇧U on Mac / Ctrl+Shift+U elsewhere (customize at chrome://extensions/shortcuts).

Your visible viewport is saved before this editor opens, together with the page title, URL, viewport, scroll position, browser, language, and timezone. Add notes and optional arrows, boxes, or pen marks.

Select saved issues to download a ZIP with original images, annotations, Markdown and JSON, or copy a text handoff for your coding agent. Export preparation is tracked; delivery and fixes are not inferred.

Everything stays in this browser profile. There is no account, server, automatic recording, console capture, or network logging. Removing the extension or clearing this site's data removes its reports. Export what you want to keep.

The standalone web inbox supports text reports and attached screenshots. Automatic page capture requires the extension. Its reports are separate from the iOS package and Mac companion format.
